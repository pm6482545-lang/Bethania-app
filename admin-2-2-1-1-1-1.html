Step 1 • Level & Daily Structure

Both tiers run 2-lesson blocks separated by short break, long break & lunch. Primary is a 7-lesson day ending with 1 lesson after lunch (no games); Junior School is an 8-lesson day ending with a games block after lunch.

Primary Block • 7 Lessons/Day

2 lessons → short break → 2 lessons → long break → 2 lessons → lunch → 1 lesson (no games block)

Junior School Block • 8 Lessons/Day

2 lessons → short break → 2 lessons → long break → 2 lessons → lunch → 2 lessons → games
